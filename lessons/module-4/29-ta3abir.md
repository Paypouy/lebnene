When to fire it: shu fi ma fi — greeting friends · wala shé 3abélé — low-energy days · 3a rasé — warm yes to a request · t2ebburné — deep affection, careful! (mothers/grandmothers; between adults only very close friends or as a joke) · ya 7aram — sympathy · niyélak — envy, friendly · wlek — surprise, calling out (rough/familiar: friends only, never elders, strangers or the boss) · mish ma32ul — disbelief · bil-3akes — polite contradiction · 3a mahlak — slow down · fallayna? — time to leave · ijit l-kahraba! — the national celebration · tawwel bélak — calm down · alla ya3tik l-3éfyé — to anyone working

## Drills
1. Situation→expression: teacher describes 12 micro-situations, student fires the right one instantly.
2. Register check: which can you say to your boss? (3a rasé ✓, t2ebburné ✗✗✗).
3. The kahraba joke: when the power cuts mid-lesson (it will), the student must react in Arabic only.

## Role-play variations
- Friend gathering: teacher + (ideally) one guest native speaker, expressions flying. Student must land ≥5 naturally.

- Anki deck 29. Field work: use 3 expressions on real Lebanese people this week, report reactions.
